Privacy Policy

1. POPIA Compliance (South Africa)

LocalFix operates in alignment with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. As the responsible party, we lawfully collect data necessary to provide our services. This includes:

  • Names and contact information (mobile numbers, email addresses).
  • Location data (GPS coordinates, property addresses) for service matching.
  • Identity documents (for Providers seeking verification).

Users have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of their personal data at any time.

2. Data Retention & Security

We process ID documents strictly for the purpose of verification. Raw RSA identity documents uploaded for manual verification are inspected securely. Once the verification process is complete, these raw document files are permanently deleted from our active servers. We only retain the verification status.

3. Third-Party Processing

To operate our platform securely and efficiently, LocalFix utilizes necessary technical infrastructure provided by trusted third parties. This includes secure database hosting and network infrastructure (such as Cloudflare tunnels/proxies) to protect data in transit and at rest.

Account & Data Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal data. You can completely delete your account and all associated personal data at any time directly through the LocalFix mobile app.

How to delete your account in the app:

  1. Open the LocalFix app and log in.
  2. Navigate to Settings.
  3. Tap on Account & Privacy.
  4. Select Delete Entire Account and confirm your choice.

Note: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your personal information, service history, and preferences will be wiped from our systems in accordance with our data retention policies and Apple/Google mandatory deletion requirements.
